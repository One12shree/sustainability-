Dear friends,

Selling good news is difficult and we really appreciate your interest and support. The Sustainabilist is now entirely digital, and we want to have you involved! We want to create a new network of like-minded individuals who are passionate about the environment and have a story to tell. The novel platform will enable the sharing of opinions, ideas, and best practices with the scope of increasing knowledge and awareness about the environment, resource consumption, and the vast number of means to mitigate climate change.

This is a growing effort where you can build a name and a reputation for yourself and all the areas where UAE and the region will aggregate sustainability-related knowledge. How can you be part of it?

Simple! Submit your stories to us. We will publish the best ones on our website and social media platforms.

Write to us if you want to know more or go directly online and submit your stories.

Best,
The Sustainabilist Team
